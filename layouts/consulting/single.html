While primarily focused on running my own companies, I occasionally take on select consulting and advisory engagements where I can make a meaningful impact. Most of my career has been spent putting Linux devices in the field and keeping them running there. With Screenly, I built a platform that powers 10,000+ Raspberry Pi-based screens worldwide for customers like NASA, Lowe's, and Capital One, and I maintain Anthias, the most popular open-source digital signage project on GitHub. That means I've lived through the parts of IoT that don't show up in the vendor pitch decks: SD cards dying in the field, OTA updates that brick devices, boards going end-of-life mid-rollout, and security teams asking what's actually running on a fleet nobody can physically reach.

I help companies navigate that journey — from choosing hardware and an update strategy, to getting a pilot into real production, to keeping a fleet patched and compliant for the next decade. Alongside the device work, I advise on cloud architecture and software supply chain security; as a co-lead for one of CISA's SBOM tiger teams, I've helped shape the standards that connected-product companies now have to meet.

How I Can Help

From Prototype to Production Fleet

A device that works on your desk is not a product. I help teams close the gap — hardware and OS choices that survive procurement and the field, provisioning that scales past manual imaging, an update path you can trust, and the monitoring you'll wish you had the first time a rollout goes sideways.

IoT Landscape & Architecture Reviews

The IoT tooling space is crowded, and a lot of it is solving someone else's problem. I offer an independent read on where your architecture will hurt, which platforms and OTA strategies actually fit your constraints, and what it will cost you to live with that decision for the next decade.

Device Security, SBOM & CRA Readiness

Connected products are now regulated products. I assess device and infrastructure security end to end — boot chain, update mechanism, backend, and CI/CD — and help you produce the SBOMs and processes the CRA expects. Having co-led CISA's SBOM tiger team, I focus on practical, actionable recommendations that balance security with shipping.

Strategic & Investor Advisory

I provide guidance on architecture decisions, technology selection, and scaling strategies, drawing on real experience building products like Screenly and YippieMove. For investors, I run technical due diligence on hardware and IoT companies, where the risk usually sits in the parts that never get demoed.
